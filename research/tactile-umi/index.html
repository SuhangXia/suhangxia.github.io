Tactile UMI

How can hand-held visuotactile demonstrations become inspectable, robot-ready trajectories?

I collected hand-held demonstrations with Quest motion, wrist RGB, dual-fingertip tactile video, and gripper state; calibrated and synchronised these streams into auditable camera-relative 7D actions. UniForce was first pretrained on Taichi-simulated indentation, then fine-tuned on real tactile presses; its frozen contact tokens conditioned a Diffusion Policy evaluated on a Franka FR3. The 222-episode corpus is distinct from the 77-demonstration controlled subset. Robot execution uses a Robotiq gripper and Polymetis impedance control.

System architecture · Source-grounded workflow

One system, four sensor streams, one robot-ready timeline.

Tactile UMI treats calibration and data quality as part of the learning system—not as hidden setup. Human demonstrations remain inspectable from acquisition through alignment, curation, representation learning, and Franka FR3 deployment.

  1. 01

    Calibrate the geometry and clocks

    Independent camera intrinsics, FR3 eye-in-hand calibration, UMI–Quest hand–eye calibration, a gripper-width lookup table, and measured stream offsets establish one explicit coordinate and timing contract.

  2. 02

    Record first, align second

    Wrist RGB, two tactile cameras, Quest motion, and gripper state retain their native timestamps in raw H5. Alignment and camera-relative pose derivation are reproducible post-processing steps.

  3. 03

    Audit before policy training

    CLI checks and web review inspect streams, timing, and trajectory segments before export to the Zarr dataset consumed by training.

UniForce · Two-stage tactile representation learning

Simulation first. Physical contact second.

UniForce is not trained directly on UMI demonstrations alone. A Taichi-generated loading-only dataset establishes the tactile representation; paired real-sensor presses then adapt it to the physical fingertips. The adapted encoder is frozen before Diffusion Policy training.

Methods & data details+

Geometry and reuse

With parent–child notation, A_T_B maps B-frame coordinates into A. The demonstration chain is W_T_C = W_T_Q · Q_T_C; camera and TCP poses remain distinct. Robot execution uses B_T_F, F_T_C and F_T_TCP. Quest/Unity axis conversion is a separate change of basis, not a hand–eye extrinsic.

All 40 recorded pose/board observations were reconstructed using the stored PARK solution. Axis-wise population SD is 1.67 / 3.09 / 1.91 mm. A new deterministic consistency check fits 32 poses and holds out every fifth pose; the eight held-out board positions have a median 4.38 mm distance from the training-board median. These are internal consistency checks.

The two collection days share the calibrated setup, as confirmed by the experimenter. The 0619 aligned files embed the matrix; 0623 uses the common Zarr export configuration. Serialized matrices agree within 10⁻⁷. The five-point UMI LUT maps pixel separation to physical gap; a separate Robotiq LUT maps that gap to driver position.

Latency measurement

The accepted calibration contains 437 ArUco detections and 1,814 Quest samples. The local implementation sign-adjusts the selected axis, interpolates Quest at camera time + δ, smooths with a one-sample Gaussian, derives velocity and minimizes normalized MSE. The recorded optimum is δ = −56.738 ms; timestamps are mapped as arrival − configured latency.

Before/after plots use the same recorded samples, axis limits and normalization. The full recording remains visible below a labelled detail. Tactile-camera flicker correlation is a separate calibration; offsets follow the recorded session/slot metadata. exUMI §3.2 and Appendix A.2 ↗

Dataset timing checks

The read-only analysis covers 223 raw episodes and the existing export of 222 episodes / 135,952 policy steps. Raw host timestamps have no adjacent duplicates or backwards steps. Exported tactile windows have no out-of-episode indices or timestamps after their policy reference. This checks mapped-time indexing; read/receive timestamps do not log the completion of all preprocessing or a policy decision deadline.

Six-frame windows are drawn from aligned tactile records. Source reuse is 15.9% on the left and 38.2% on the right. Maximum latest-frame mapped ages are 90.2 / 178.2 ms. Episode-start padding retains validity masks. The original export and historical experiment results are unchanged.

The illustrative selector chooses the closest observation within ±60 ms of t_ref, subject to its assumed available time ≤ t_decision. States received after t_ref but before the decision can support an appropriate interpolation; taking each stream’s newest frame alone does not establish synchronization.

Action labels and execution

The export stores absolute future camera targets with action_target_offset_steps = 1. The dataset converts these into relative SE(3) actions; future targets are supervision, not observation inputs. The empirically selected action-chunk index 1 is separate from that label offset and is not a measured end-to-end delay. A TCP bridge maps camera-relative targets to Franka FR3 and Robotiq commands while Polymetis runs impedance control.
