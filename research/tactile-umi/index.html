Tactile UMI

How can hand-held visuotactile demonstrations become inspectable, robot-ready trajectories?

A geometrically and temporally calibrated pipeline that aligns Quest motion, wrist RGB, dual-fingertip tactile video, and gripper state. Raw demonstrations remain auditable before conversion into camera-relative 7D actions; frozen UniForce contact tokens then condition a Diffusion Policy deployed on a Franka FR3.

GitHub repository ↗UniForce · arXiv ↗

System architecture · Source-grounded workflow

One system, four sensor streams, one robot-ready timeline.

Tactile UMI treats calibration and data quality as part of the learning system—not as hidden setup. Human demonstrations remain inspectable from acquisition through alignment, curation, representation learning, and Franka FR3 deployment.

  1. 01

    Calibrate the geometry and clocks

    Independent camera intrinsics, FR3 eye-in-hand calibration, UMI–Quest hand–eye calibration, a gripper-width lookup table, and measured stream offsets establish one explicit coordinate and timing contract.

  2. 02

    Record first, align second

    Wrist RGB, two tactile cameras, Quest motion, and gripper state retain their native timestamps in raw H5. Alignment and camera-relative pose derivation are reproducible post-processing steps.

  3. 03

    Audit before policy training

    CLI and web review expose missing streams, timing faults, and invalid episodes. Only accepted trajectories are promoted to the clean Zarr dataset consumed by training.

Evidence boundary

The observed direction is encouraging but not statistically significant at this sample size (two-sided Fisher exact test, p = 0.447). This preliminary evaluation does not establish broad generalisation, cloth-manipulation performance, stable placement, or a causal mechanism for the improvement. The separate π0.5 integration is retained as systems evidence and is not folded into this 34-versus-34 comparison.
