VTLA for Cloth Sorting

When is a second touch worth its sensing cost for estimating fabric thickness and areal mass?

Fabric-Omni records 200 fabrics through two-sided RGB, GelSight, and force-aligned presses. TouchUntilCertain predicts thickness and GSM, then spends a second touch only when the first appears unreliable.

Dataset / Fabric-Omni

200 fabrics. Two sides. One traceable record.

Catalogue provenance, RGB, GelSight video, and ≈500 Hz ATI force are linked press by press.

Method / TouchUntilCertain

Predict once. Re-touch only when needed.

Frozen tactile tokens estimate thickness and GSM; three target-free signals decide STOP or RETOUCH.
